        About Next Read

        How it works

        Made by Anna Wang to help kids find their next favorite book.

        📚 Two ways to pick books

        Matching tags: every book on Open Library has subject tags like "dragons" or "friendship". Next Read finds popular books that share the most specific tags with yours.

        ✨ AI, similar meaning: a small AI model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) reads each book's description and turns it into 384 numbers that capture what it's about. Books with similar numbers have similar meaning, even if their tags are different.

        🛡️ Safe for kids

        Next Read works out who a book is for from its tags, its reading level ("Grade 4", "Ages 8-12"), its page count, and the author's other books.

        • Picture books only get picture books. Children's books only get children's books.
        • Murder, horror and grown-up topics are blocked for kids. Scary books only appear if your own book is scary (Goosebumps readers can get spooky kids' books, never Stephen King).
        • The AI only re-orders books that already passed these checks, so it can't bring adult books back.
        • Tested: an automatic safety check runs 18 kids' books. Without the filter, 132 of 205 picks had problems. With it: 0.

        🐛 The bug I fixed

        Kids who read Encyclopedia Brown were getting Murder on the Orient Express. Why? Lots of adult books have one kids' edition, so Open Library tags them "juvenile". Next Read saw that one tag and thought they were kids' books.

        →

        The fix: a book only counts as a kids' book if it has several kids' tags (real kids' books have lots, adult books have one or two), plus reading-level and topic checks. Now Encyclopedia Brown gets Nancy Drew, the Hardy Boys and Enola Holmes.

        🔒 Private by design

        • No accounts and no sign-in. No names needed.
        • Your book log, reading time, pet and blind-test votes stay in your own browser. They're never uploaded.
        • The AI runs on your device. Book descriptions are never sent to an AI company.
        • The only things that leave your device: book searches to Open Library (a free library catalog), and a one-time download of the AI model from Hugging Face.
