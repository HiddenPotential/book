- Bring this sheet and a pen.

## Questions

### About reluctant readers
1. Which grades have the most kids who don't like reading?
2. What do reluctant readers usually say when they come to the library?
3. How do you help a kid who says "I don't know what to read"?
4. What kinds of books get reluctant readers interested? (series, graphic novels, funny books, short books, nonfiction…)
5. What makes a kid give up on a book?

### About recommendations
6. When a kid liked a book, how do you pick the next one for them?
7. Which is more important for a next book: the same topic, the same feeling, or the same reading level?
8. Are there books you would never recommend to a certain age? How do you decide?
9. Have you seen a recommendation website or app suggest something wrong for a kid's age?

### Try Next Read together
10. Search for a book a reluctant reader at our school liked. Are the recommendations good? Which ones would you change?
11. Try the AI method and the tag-matching method for the same book. Which list is better, and why?
12. Is anything confusing for a kid using it alone?
13. Would the reading timer or the reading pet motivate kids? What would make it better?

### Safety and privacy
14. Is it OK that the book log is only saved on the device, with no accounts?
15. Is there anything you'd want to check before kids at our school use it?

### Testing
16. Could I run a blind test with some students (they pick between two lists without knowing which is the AI)? How many students, and what would I need permission for?
